# Known acquisition / merger map for lenders in the HMDA all-lenders universe

This is a researched map of public, identifiable acquisition or merger relationships among lender names that appear in the broader HMDA all-lenders universe. It is **not an exhaustive legal-entity merger database** for every small bank, credit union, mortgage branch platform, or failed-bank receivership from 2007-2025. It is meant as a practical lookup file for the major and clearly sourced relationships that can explain why lender names disappear, reappear, or roll into another lender series.

The table is organized with **one acquired or merged-in institution per row**. The acquirer or successor is repeated where one company absorbed multiple institutions. The year column uses the acquisition, merger-completion, failure-transfer, or brand-lineage year shown by the cited source.

## How to use this map

Use this file as a lookup companion to the churn files. If a lender disappears from the annual lender list, check whether it is in the acquired-institution column here. If it is, the disappearance may reflect acquisition, merger, brand conversion, or platform absorption rather than a simple business failure or organic exit.

For a more exhaustive version, the next step would be to join HMDA respondent IDs / LEIs against regulatory merger histories, FDIC institution history, NCUA credit union history, and company press-release records. That would be a separate data-engineering project because the all-lenders universe contains more than 17,000 canonical lender series.
