# Purchase, refinancing and mortgage-rate companion, 2007–2025

Prepared 26 September 2026. **Read this before the market commentary.** This companion adds loan-purpose detail to the existing [annual top-25 rankings](top_25_lenders_by_year_2007_2025.md), without changing that file. The interpretation is in [Lender market dynamics](lender_market_dynamics_commentary_2007_2025.md).

## What these tables measure

- **Applications:** HMDA action codes 1–5, including originated, approved but not taken, denied, withdrawn and incomplete applications. These are reported records, not unique households.
- **Loans made / originations:** action code 1 only. Purchased loans, action 6, are excluded from these totals. A loan purchased from another lender is different from a loan to purchase a home.
- **Purchase:** purpose code 1. **Total refinancing:** code 3 through 2017; codes 31 and 32 from 2018. **Cash-out:** code 32, a subset of total refinancing, not an additional category to sum.
- **Other:** every remaining purpose—home improvement, other purpose and not applicable. It does not automatically mean a HELOC or a second mortgage. Those characteristics are examined separately below.
- **Rank:** position by total applications across all purposes, matching the existing report. A smaller rank number means a higher position. Tables retain each year's top 25, rather than fixing a survivor cohort.
- **Shares:** loan counts, not dollars. Purchase mix means the lender's purchase originations divided by its own total originations. Segment share means the lender's originations in that purpose divided by industry originations in the same purpose.

The older HMDA schema does not distinguish cash-out from other refinancing, so 2007–2017 cash-out cells are **N/A**, not zero. Definitions and reporting coverage changed in 2018, including mandatory open-end reporting for covered institutions. Long-run changes therefore combine market movement with changes in coverage. See the CFPB's [review of revised HMDA data points](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_data-points_updated-review-hmda_report.pdf) and [Regulation C amendments](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/final-rules/regulation-c-home-mortgage-disclosure-act/).

## 4. What changed between the boom and 2025?

| Purpose | 2021 made | 2023 made | 2025 made | 2021–25 change | 2023–25 change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Purchase | 5,359,280 | 3,448,703 | 3,592,033 | -33.0% | +4.2% |
| Total refinance | 8,665,079 | 1,083,700 | 1,995,427 | -77.0% | +84.1% |
| Other | 972,949 | 1,159,323 | 1,240,431 | +27.5% | +7.0% |

This is the key control for interpreting lender ranks: the refinance segment contracted much more than purchase lending. A lender can improve its position within refinancing and still lose share of the entire market. The “other” bucket grew, making home-equity-oriented institutions more visible in an all-purpose ranking.

### Separate within-purpose share changes from the changing market mix

The following exact arithmetic decomposition uses three exhaustive groups: purchase, total refinance and other. It answers how much of the overall share change comes from changes in a lender's share **inside each group**, and how much comes from changes in the industry's weighting of those groups. It is descriptive, not a causal estimate of customer wins.

For each group, let `s` be the lender's share of that group and `w` the group's share of all industry originations. With 0 = 2021 and 1 = 2025:

- Within-purpose component = sum of `(s1 − s0) × (w0 + w1) / 2`.
- Market-mix component = sum of `(w1 − w0) × (s0 + s1) / 2`.
- The two components sum exactly to the change in overall share before rounding.

No lender-specific interest-rate comparison is made: comparing unadjusted loan rates would confound product type, lien, term, credit risk, points and timing. The external PMMS benchmark is sufficient for broad cycle context; a pricing study would require a separately matched loan sample. The report covers 2007–2025, **19 calendar years**, not a completed 2026 observation.
