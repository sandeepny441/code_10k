# Top 25 lenders by year, 2007–2025

Ranks 1–25 are assigned separately each year by total applications received. Each row is one filing institution.

**Loans made** are funded originations. **Approved applications** include loans made plus applications approved but not taken. **Approval %** = approved applications ÷ total applications.

**Share of industry loans made** = this lender’s loans made ÷ loans made by all HMDA-reporting lenders in that year.

Historical Shore Mortgage and United Shore Financial Service filings are displayed as **United Wholesale Mortgage (UWM)**. Its rank reflects each year’s application volume.
