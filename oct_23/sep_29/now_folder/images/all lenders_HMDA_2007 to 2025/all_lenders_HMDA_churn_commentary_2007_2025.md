# All-lenders HMDA churn and persistence, 2007-2025

This analysis follows every lender-year aggregate in the all-lenders HMDA package, ranked by loans funded within each year. It is the full-dataset counterpart to the top-500 churn commentary.

The lender identity is based on normalized lender names, with obvious aliases combined where they matter for this file, including United Wholesale Mortgage / United Shore as UWM and Rocket Mortgage / Quicken Loans as one series. It does not attempt to fully merge every merger, acquisition, or legal-entity restructuring in the mortgage industry.

## How to read the categories

- **Remained**: appeared in one year and also in the next year.

- **Went away**: appeared in one year but did not appear in the next year.

- **Entrant**: appeared in the next year but not the prior year.

- **New joiner**: an entrant whose first observed appearance is that year.

- **Came back**: an entrant that had appeared before, disappeared for at least one year, and then returned.

- **Consistent**: appeared in all 19 years.

- **Old runner**: began in 2007 and appeared in at least 10 years.

- **Flip-flopper**: moved in and out across multiple spells, or returned after a gap while having eight or fewer total appearances.

- **Guest**: appeared in exactly one year.

## What the movement says

Across the full all-lenders file, the average year kept **4887** of the prior year lenders and lost **992**. The incoming side averaged **529** first-time new joiners and **283** comeback lenders per year. Because this is not capped at the top 500, it captures far more small-lender churn and one-year participation than the leaderboard-only file.


The largest exit wave in this all-lenders file is **2017 → 2018**, when **2,101** prior-year lenders disappeared from the next year list. The largest entrant wave is **2017 → 2018**, with **2,006** entrants. The largest comeback count is **2019 → 2020**, with **768** returning lenders.
