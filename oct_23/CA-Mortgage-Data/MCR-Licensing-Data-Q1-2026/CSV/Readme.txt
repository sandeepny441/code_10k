NMLS Mortgage Industry Report, 2026 Q1 Update
Created 6/8/2026

Introduction:
This zip archive contains six comma-separated values (.csv) files with data derived from the NMLS, including Mortgage Call Report (MCR) data. Each file is organized by state regulatory agency, year, and quarter. The files contain data starting from 2013Q1.  Any revisions or editions submitted by companies for past quarters have been included in this update to the data set.

Files:
Forward Loans by Business Line.csv
Forward Loans Closed and Funded by Purpose.csv
Forward Loans Closed and Funded by Type.csv
Loan Applications Directly Received.csv
Mortgage License Activity.csv
Reverse Loans by Business Line.csv

File Descriptions:
Forward Loans by Business Line.csv
Forward loans are those where borrowers make regular payments until all the principal is paid off (i.e. not a reverse loan). All loans were closed and funded in the quarter in which they were reported.  There are three possible business lines:
•	Brokered: Loans where a company took the application but did not fund the loan.
•	Retail: Loans where a company took the application and funded the loan.
•	Wholesale: Loans where a company did not take the application, but did fund the loan.
Note that a loan may be included in the loan volume for both brokered and wholesale loans.  

Forward Loans Closed and Funded by Purpose.csv
There are three possible loan purposes: 
•	Home Purchase
•	Refinancing
•	Home improvement
 Wholesale loans are excluded from this report to avoid double counting.

 Forward Loans Closed and Funded by Type.csv
There are four possible loan types: 
•	Conventional
•	Federal Housing Administration (FHA)-Insured
•	Veterans Administration (VA)-Guaranteed
•	Farm Service Agency (FSA) or Rural Housing Service (RHS)-Guaranteed.  
Wholesale loans are excluded from this report to avoid double counting.

Loan Applications Directly Received.csv
Loan applications directly received are applications that the company received from the borrower.  Applications received from a third party are excluded to avoid double counting.

Reverse Loans by Business Line.csv
Reverse loans are loans secured by a lien on residential real estate in which the homeowner is not required to make payments on the loan until a specific event occurs. There are three possible business lines:
•	Brokered: Loans where a company took the application but did not fund the loan.
•	Retail: Loans where a company took the application and funded the loan.
•	Wholesale: Loans where a company did not take the application, but did fund the loan.
Note that a loan may be included in the loan volume for both brokered and wholesale loans.

Mortgage License Activity.csv
Mortgage licensing activity includes applications that were new, approved, denied, withdrawn, revoked, surrendered, or terminated during each quarter. The activity data is broken down by entity type (company, branch, and individual).
